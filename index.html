How it works

Three small contracts. No server, no keeper bot: the reactive contract is the one keeping watch.

1

Check in

Now and then, one small transaction from your wallet says you are alive.

2

The silence is counted

A contract on Reactive Network wakes on a schedule and counts the days since your last sign.

3

Warning, then grace

After 30 days of silence it warns. For the next 7 days, a single check-in cancels everything.

4

Heirs paid

Still silent? The vault checks once more, releases, and pays your heirs by fixed shares.

Your coins never move into the vault. It only holds an allowance, which you can revoke at any time.
